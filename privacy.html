Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Bangkok Social ("we") runs this website to list social dance events in Bangkok and let dancers RSVP. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google or LINE: your name, profile photo, and (if the provider shares it) your email address. We use these only to show organisers who has RSVP'd.
  • Your RSVPs: which events you said you're going to, and whether you checked in at the door.
  • Your location: only if you tap "Near me", and only in your browser to work out distances. It is never sent to us or stored.

Browsing the timetable doesn't require an account, and we don't collect anything about you when you only browse.

Who can see it

Other dancers only see how many people are going. Event organisers can see the names and photos of people who RSVP'd to their events, so they can prepare and check people in. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and login) on servers in Singapore. The website is hosted by Vercel. Maps are shown with OpenStreetMap.

Deleting your data

You can cancel any RSVP yourself at any time. To delete your account and all your data, email us at the address below and we'll do it within 30 days.
